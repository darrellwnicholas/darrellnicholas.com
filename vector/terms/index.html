Vector · Terms of Use

The deal, in plain English.

These are the terms for using Vector, the roulette tracking app for iPhone. I have written them the way I would say them out loud, because terms nobody reads protect nobody. Using the app means you accept what is on this page.

Last updated 1 September 2026.

What Vector is

Vector is a tracking and analysis tool. You type in the numbers a roulette wheel produces, and it keeps the record — the spins, the distances between them, and what has happened at the table so far.

That is the whole of it. Vector does not accept wagers, does not handle money, and is not a gambling service. It has no connection to any casino and no involvement in anything you do at one. It is a notebook that does arithmetic.

You have to be 18 or over

Vector is for adults only. By using it you confirm you are at least 18 years old, and that gambling is legal where you are. Knowing the law where you live is your responsibility, not mine — it varies enormously and I cannot advise you on it.

What Vector cannot do

This is the most important section on the page, so it is not buried at the bottom.

Roulette outcomes are random

Every spin is independent of the one before it. The wheel has no memory. Nothing in the history of a table changes the odds of the next number, and no amount of recorded data changes that fact.

There is no guarantee of winnings

Vector makes no promise that you will win anything, and I make no claim that it gives you an edge over the house. It does not. If you are buying it expecting to profit, please do not buy it.

My own testing did not beat chance

I have backtested the method against recorded spins, and the results did not beat chance. I would rather tell you that here than have you find it out with your own money. Anyone who tells you a tracker beats roulette is selling something.

So what are you paying for?

The tracking and the record — a fast, tidy way to log a table and see what it has actually done. That is a real thing and it is what the price is for. It is not a promise about the next spin.

The app is provided as is

Vector comes with no warranty of any kind. I have built it carefully and I use it myself, but I cannot promise it will be free of bugs, always available, or fit for any particular purpose. Software written by one person sometimes gets things wrong.

Every decision you make while using Vector is yours. Any money you stake, win or lose is yours. To the fullest extent the law allows, I am not liable for gambling losses or for any other loss that follows from using the app. If that is not a risk you want to carry, do not use it to inform bets.

Buying, subscriptions and renewals

Recording spins, the distances and the accuracy record stay free. The prediction feature is what is sold, and every purchase goes through Apple. Apple takes the payment and handles the billing — I never see your card or payment details.

Monthly subscription

Auto-renewing. It renews each month unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

Annual subscription

Auto-renewing. It renews each year unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

Lifetime unlock

A one-time purchase, not a subscription. Nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. You can restore it on your other devices with the same Apple Account.

Subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account settings, not inside Vector — that is where you go to cancel or to change plan, and cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple under their terms, because Apple is who you paid.

Most countries have a free, confidential gambling helpline, and they are staffed by people who have heard it all before and will not lecture you. Please use one. No feature in this app matters more than that.
