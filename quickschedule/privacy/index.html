QuickSchedule · Privacy

QuickSchedule collects nothing and sends nothing.

That is the whole policy. The rest of this page explains what it means and why it can be said that plainly, because a privacy policy that takes ten minutes to read is usually hiding something.

Last updated 2 September 2026.

There is nowhere for your data to go

QuickSchedule has no server. It contains no networking code of any kind — not for analytics, not for advertising, not for crash reports, not for anything. There are no third-party SDKs in it. There is no account to create and no login, because there is nothing to log in to.

This is not a promise about careful handling. It is simpler than that: the app has no way to send anything anywhere, so nothing about you reaches me. In Apple's App Privacy questions this is the answer marked Data Not Collected, and it is the truthful one.

What is stored, and where

Everything QuickSchedule records stays in the app's own storage on your iPhone.

Your schedule

The days of your week, the shifts on each of them, their names and their start and finish times.

Your employee list

The names you type in, and an email address for anyone you choose to add one for. This is the only personal information the app holds, you enter it yourself, and it never leaves your device unless you send a schedule.

Nothing else

No location, no contacts, no photos, no device identifiers, no usage tracking. QuickSchedule never asks for any of it, so it does not have it to lose.

When you share a schedule

Tapping the Q turns your schedule into a PDF and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet. From there you decide where it goes — Messages, Mail, AirDrop, Files, a printer. That document contains the names and shift times you entered.

Nothing is sent automatically and nothing is sent to me. The share only happens because you chose a destination, and whatever you pick is between you and that app.

Your data is yours, and only yours

Those files live on your device and are included in your normal iPhone backup, so they travel with you to a new phone. Nobody else can see them, including me. They are never transmitted, sold, shared, or used for advertising, and there is no analytics code watching what you tap.

Deleting the app deletes the lot. That also means I cannot recover it for you afterwards — I never had a copy. It is the honest trade-off that comes with not collecting anything.

Children, and changes to this policy

QuickSchedule is a scheduling tool for small businesses. It is not directed at children and it knowingly collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version goes here with a new date at the top. If QuickSchedule ever started collecting anything, this page would say so before it shipped.
