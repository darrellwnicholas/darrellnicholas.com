About

Darrell Nicholas has spent over a decade in network administration and hands-on field service — including years inside ATM and point-of-sale systems for national companies — before teaching himself iOS, Android, and web development.

Outside of writing, he builds free, ad-free software for the people around him, including a small library of games made for an elderly neighbor, safe from the predatory design most app-store versions run on.
